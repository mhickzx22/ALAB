FAQ

Good to know.

How do I book a game or court?

Open the ALAB app to see the live schedule, available games and open courts, then reserve your slot.

Do I need my own paddle?

Not at all. Don’t have a paddle yet? You can rent one from us and enjoy the game hassle-free.

I am new to pickleball. Can I join?

Absolutely. Beginners are always welcome. Come as you are, learn at your own pace, and enjoy the game.
